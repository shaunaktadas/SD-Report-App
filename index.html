Blending Calculation Formula

Target Quantity Equation:
Q₂ = Qₜ - Q₁

Required New Material LSF (L₂):
L₂ = [(Lₜ × Qₜ) − (L₁ × Q₁)] / Q₂

Required New Material MgO (M₂):
M₂ = [(Mₜ × Qₜ) − (M₁ × Q₁)] / Q₂
